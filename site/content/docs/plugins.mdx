A plugin adds a panel or a theme to better-vault: a panel is a small page that runs inside the app, and a theme changes the app's colours.

## Install a plugin

<Steps>

### Add it

Open **Settings → Plugins** and choose **Install**, then pick a `.bvplugin.json` file. You can also let an AI agent build one for you; [Creator mode](/docs/creator-mode) explains how.

### Choose what it may use

A new plugin arrives turned off. Its card lists what it asks for, such as **Read notebooks' sources** or **Create notes**. Untick anything you don't want to give it.

### Turn it on

Turn on the plugin's switch. Its panels appear in the **Plugins** group of the Studio sheet, and open in the Studio tab with the notebook you're in. A theme recolours the app at once, in light and dark mode.

</Steps>

## What a plugin can do

| Permission                  | Lets the plugin                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Read your notebooks**     | List your notebooks and how many sources each has |
| **Read notebooks' sources** | List a notebook's sources and read their text     |
| **Read your notes**         | List your notes and read them                     |
| **Read your skills**        | Read your skills and their instructions           |
| **Read your calendar**      | List your events between two days                 |
| **Create notes**            | Create notes in the current vault                 |
| **Add calendar events**     | Add events to your calendar                       |

A plugin never sees your settings or API keys, whatever it asks for.

## How plugins are kept safe

A plugin's panel runs in a sandbox. It can't reach any server, so it can't load scripts or pictures from the internet or send your data anywhere. It can't read the app's storage or pages either. The only way it gets your data is by asking better-vault, which answers only what you allowed. If a panel tries to leave its page, better-vault stops it.

A theme can only set the app's colour tokens, to plain colours, and runs no code.

When a plugin is updated and asks for more than before, it turns off until you look at it again. Plugins in a backup, or synced from another device, also arrive turned off. Install plugins you trust, as you would any app.

## Sync

With a Pro or Max plan, your plugins sync to your other devices along with your skills. [Subscription](/docs/subscription) explains how sync works.
