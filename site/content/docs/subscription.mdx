better-vault works fully without an account; an optional subscription adds Better Vault AI, Better Vault Cloud and the sync of your skills and plugins.

<Screenshot name="subscription" alt="The Subscription sheet in Settings, with the Pro and Max plans" />

## What the plans include

|                                    | Free | Pro           | Max           |
| ---------------------------------- | ---- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Price                              | $0   | $4.99 a month | $9.99 a month |
| Your own API keys and local models | Yes  | Yes           | Yes           |
| Better Vault AI, no key needed     | No   | Yes           | Yes           |
| AI credits a month                 | —    | 1,250         | 3,500         |
| Better Vault Cloud                 | —    | 20 GB         | 50 GB         |
| Skills and plugins on every device | —    | Yes           | Yes           |

Credits measure how much Better Vault AI you use; a long answer over many sources uses more than a short one. When they run out, Better Vault AI pauses until next month unless you turn on **Extra usage**, which costs $2 per 1,000 credits up to a monthly limit you choose. [Pricing](/pricing) compares the plans in full.

## Create an account and subscribe

<Steps>

### Sign in

Open **Settings → Subscription** and choose **Create account** or **Sign in**. A sign-in page opens: in a window on the web, or in your browser for the desktop app. Use Google or an email and password, then allow better-vault to use the account.

### Choose a plan

Choose **Get Pro** or **Get Max**. Dodo Payments, which handles payments, opens the checkout. When you come back, better-vault thanks you and offers to switch the prompt to Better Vault AI.

### Use Better Vault AI

Pick **Better Vault** in the prompt's model picker. Its logo shows that the question uses your plan.

</Steps>

The first time you open the app, the welcome screens offer the same steps, and **Use without an account** skips them.

## Manage or cancel

Settings → Subscription shows your plan, the credits and storage used and left, and when they renew. **Manage or cancel** opens the billing portal of Dodo Payments, where you can change plan, see invoices or cancel; a cancelled plan lasts until the end of the month you paid for.

## Sync skills and plugins

With a plan, your skills and plugins sync through your account, so signing in on another computer or browser brings them back. It is on by default; **Sync skills and plugins** in Settings → Subscription turns it off and shows when they last synced. Edits and deletes travel too, and when the same skill changed in two places the newer edit wins.

A plugin that arrives from another device is checked again and stays turned off until you turn it on there, because what it may read is your choice on each device. Notebooks, notes and API keys never sync: they stay where you made them.

## Privacy

Your notebooks, notes and API keys stay on your device and never go to Better Vault. Only the questions you send to Better Vault AI, the files you choose to put in Better Vault Cloud, and, with sync on, your skills and plugins reach our servers. The account itself is your email, your name and your username.
