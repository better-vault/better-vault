A notebook is a workspace for one topic, course or project, with its own sources, chats and Studio outputs.

## The notebook screen

The buttons at the top left open **Settings** and **Create**, which opens the [Studio](/docs/studio). In the Chat tab, a **History** button sits beside them. The notebook's name is at the top centre; click it to switch notebooks or to create, rename or delete one.

The tabs at the top right switch between **Chat**, **Sources** and **Studio**. In the Chat tab, the prompt bar at the bottom holds the model picker and the Sources and Web switch.

## Create a notebook

Choose **New notebook** on the notebooks page, which is the app's home, or from the name menu. Your next step is to add [sources](/docs/sources).

## Chats and History

A notebook can hold several chats. Open **History** to see them grouped by day. From there you can open an older chat, rename or delete it, or start a **New chat**.

## Delete a notebook

Open the name menu and choose **Delete notebook**. This removes its sources, chats and outputs for good, so export a backup in **Settings → Data** first if you may want them back.
