Flashcards turn your sources into question-and-answer cards for testing yourself.

## Options

| Option              | Choices                                                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Number of cards** | Fewer (about 10), standard (about 20) or more (about 35)    |
| **Difficulty**      | Easy, medium or hard                                        |

## Study the cards

Flip a card with a click or the keyboard, then mark it **knew it** or **still learning**. The tallies show your progress, and you can shuffle the deck at any time.

## Download

Download the cards as Word, in a two-column table, or as CSV, which imports into Anki and most flashcard apps.
