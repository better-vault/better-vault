**Flashcards** turn your sources into question-and-answer cards.

## Options

- **Number of cards:** fewer (about 10), standard (about 20) or more (about 35).
- **Difficulty:** easy, medium or hard.

## Studying

Flip a card with a click or the keyboard, mark it **knew it** or **still learning**, and shuffle the deck. The tallies show your progress.

## Download

Word (a two-column table) or CSV, which imports into Anki and most flashcard apps.
