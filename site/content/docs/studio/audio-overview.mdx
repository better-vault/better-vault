An Audio Overview is a conversation about your sources between two hosts, like a short podcast. Your chat model writes the script from the selected sources, and ElevenLabs speaks it with the two voices you choose.

## Before you start

Audio Overviews need an ElevenLabs API key. Open **Settings → AI providers → ElevenLabs** and paste your key; it is used only for speech, so your chat model stays as it is.

## Options

| Option          | Choices                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Format**      | Deep dive, brief or debate                                                |
| **Length**      | Short (about 3 minutes), medium (about 7) or long (about 12)              |
| **Hosts**       | Two voices from your ElevenLabs account; their names are the hosts' names |
| **Voice model** | `eleven_v3` or `eleven_v4`, which speak both hosts as one dialogue        |

Each voice has a play button in the dialog, so you can hear a sample before you choose. The ElevenLabs key is sent with this request only.

## Listen

The player shows a wave that moves with the voice of whoever is speaking, the recording's shape as a seek bar, and buttons to skip 15 seconds or change the speed. The transcript under it follows along; choose any line to jump there, and point at a citation mark to see the passage it draws on.

The recording is kept on your device with the notebook, and included in backups.

## Download

Download the recording as MP3, or the transcript as Markdown.
