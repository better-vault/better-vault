---

A Quiz is a set of multiple-choice questions about your sources.

## Options

| Option                  | Choices                                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Number of questions** | Fewer (5), standard (10) or more (20)      |
| **Difficulty**          | Easy, medium or hard                       |

## Take the quiz

Each question has a hint you can reveal. After you answer, an explanation of the right answer appears, and your score is shown at the end.

## Download

Download the quiz as Word, with the questions first and an answer key on its own page, or as Markdown.
