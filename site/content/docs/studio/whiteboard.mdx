---

The Whiteboard is a blank board with shapes, arrows, a pen, text and pictures, drawn in a hand-drawn style. It needs no model and no sources, and opens at once from **Create**.

The board fills the Studio tab and saves your edits as you go. Its colours follow light and dark mode. Open **Keyboard shortcuts** on the board to see them all; ⌘ is Ctrl on Windows and Linux.

## Annotate a figure

Choose **Annotate** on a mind map, Anatomy figure or infographic to open it on a new board, then draw over it.

## Download

Download the board as PNG, SVG or a board file (`.json`).
