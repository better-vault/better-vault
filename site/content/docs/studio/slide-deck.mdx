A Slide deck is a presentation made from your sources.

## Options

| Option     | Choices                               |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Format** | Detailed deck or Presenter slides     |
| **Length** | Short or default                      |

A **detailed deck** uses full sentences and the key facts, so it can be read without a speaker. **Presenter slides** keep short points on the slides and put what to say in the speaker notes.

The model lays out each slide as a title, section, bullets, two columns, comparison, stats, quote, timeline or closing slide.

## Present

Present the deck full screen and move between slides with the keyboard.

## Download

Download the deck as PowerPoint (`.pptx`, with speaker notes) or as PDF through the print dialog.
