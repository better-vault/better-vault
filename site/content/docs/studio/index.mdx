The Studio turns your selected sources into something you can study, share or present. Open it with **Create** at the top left of a notebook.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick a tool

Choose **Create** and pick a tool. Most tools open a dialog of options, such as length, difficulty or style.

</Step>
<Step>

### Generate

Choose **Generate**. The model reads your selected sources, and better-vault checks its result against the tool's format before saving it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the output

The output is listed under the tools and opens in the **Studio** tab, where you can rename, delete or download it.

</Step>
</Steps>

The Whiteboard works differently: it opens in your browser at once, with no model and no sources.

## The tools

| Tool                                    | What you get                                                             | Download as         |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| [Report](/docs/studio/report)           | Briefing doc, study guide, blog post or your own format, with citations | Word, Markdown      |
| [Flashcards](/docs/studio/flashcards)   | Flip cards with shuffle and "knew it / still learning" tallies           | Word, CSV (Anki)    |
| [Quiz](/docs/studio/quiz)               | Multiple-choice questions with hints, explanations and a score           | Word, Markdown      |
| [Data table](/docs/studio/data-table)   | Facts from your sources in rows and columns                              | Excel, CSV          |
| [Mind map](/docs/studio/mind-map)       | An interactive mind map, or one of 11 other diagram kinds                | PNG, SVG, Mermaid   |
| [Slide deck](/docs/studio/slide-deck)   | A detailed deck or presenter slides with speaker notes                   | PowerPoint, PDF     |
| [Infographic](/docs/studio/infographic) | A one-page picture from your provider's image model                      | PNG, original image |
| [Anatomy](/docs/studio/anatomy)         | An interactive isometric figure you can take apart                       | PNG, SVG            |
| [Whiteboard](/docs/studio/whiteboard)   | A hand-drawn style board, also for drawing over figures                  | PNG, SVG, JSON      |

better-vault does not make audio or video overviews.
