A Report is a written document made from your selected sources, with a table of contents and claims that cite your sources.

## Options

| Option           | Choices                                                                  |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Format**       | Briefing doc, Study guide, Blog post, Create your own, or a suggested format |
| **Length**       | Short, standard or long                                                  |
| **Instructions** | Anything extra the model should follow, up to 4,000 characters          |

A **briefing doc** gives an overview of your sources with key insights and quotes. A **study guide** holds a short-answer quiz, essay questions and a glossary. A **blog post** turns the most insightful takeaways into a readable article.

With **Create your own**, you describe the structure, style and tone you want. The dialog also offers a few formats that the model suggests for your particular sources.

## Download

Download the report as Word (`.docx`, with citations as footnotes) or Markdown (`.md`).
