Anatomy explains your sources through a real object. The model designs an object whose working illustrates the ideas in your sources, names its parts and assemblies, writes the steps of how it works with citations, and builds its geometry from simple shapes.

## Use the figure

Point at a part to read what it does, step through how the object works, or take it apart to see the inside.

## Options

| Option     | Choices                         |
| ---------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Detail** | Simple, standard or detailed    |

Every figure is generated for your sources; better-vault ships no ready-made figures. Parts that float or overlap are sent back to the model to fix.

## Download

Download the figure as PNG or SVG. Choose **Annotate** to draw over it on a [Whiteboard](/docs/studio/whiteboard).
