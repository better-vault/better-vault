---
title: "Data table"
description: "Collect the facts in your sources into a table whose columns the model chooses, for comparing items or listing dates, then export it to Excel or to CSV."
---

A Data table gathers facts from your sources into rows and columns, for comparing items, listing dates or building a dataset.

The model chooses the columns that fit your sources, so there are no options to set.

## Download

Download the table as Excel (`.xlsx`, with a frozen header row) or CSV.
