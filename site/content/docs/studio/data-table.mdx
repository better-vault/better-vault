---
title: "Data table"
description: "Collect the facts in your sources into a table with columns chosen by the model, and export it to Excel or CSV."
---

A **Data table** gathers facts from your sources into rows and columns, for comparing items, listing dates or building a dataset.

The model chooses the columns that fit your sources. There are no options to set.

## Download

Excel (`.xlsx`, with a frozen header row) or CSV.
