---
title: "Infographic"
description: "Create a one-page infographic of your sources with your provider's image model, from a cited brief."
---

An **Infographic** is a one-page picture that summarizes your sources. better-vault first writes a cited brief (sections, key numbers and a takeaway), then asks an image model to draw it.

## Options

- **Image model:** an OpenAI GPT Image model or a Google Gemini image model. Its key is sent with this request only.
- **Orientation:** landscape, portrait or square.
- **Style:** auto, professional, editorial, sketch note, playful, scientific or minimal.
- **Detail:** concise, standard or detailed.

The brief and its citations stay with the picture under **Details**, so you can check what it says. Pictures are drawn only by your provider's image model; better-vault uses no other image service.

## Download

PNG or the original image file. Choose **Annotate** to draw over it on a [Whiteboard](/docs/studio/whiteboard).
