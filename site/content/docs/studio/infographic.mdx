---
title: "Infographic"
description: "Create a one-page infographic that summarizes your sources, drawn by your provider's image model from a cited brief, in the orientation and style you pick."
---

An Infographic is a one-page picture that summarizes your sources. better-vault first writes a cited brief, with sections, key numbers and a takeaway, then asks an image model to draw it.

## Options

| Option          | Choices                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Image model** | An OpenAI GPT Image model or a Google Gemini image model                  |
| **Orientation** | Landscape, portrait or square                                             |
| **Style**       | Auto, professional, editorial, sketch note, playful, scientific or minimal |
| **Detail**      | Concise, standard or detailed                                             |

The image model's key is sent with this request only. Pictures are drawn only by your provider's image model; better-vault uses no other image service.

The brief and its citations stay with the picture under **Details**, so you can check what it says.

## Download

Download the picture as PNG or as the original image file. Choose **Annotate** to draw over it on a [Whiteboard](/docs/studio/whiteboard).
