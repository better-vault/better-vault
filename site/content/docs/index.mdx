---
title: "What is better-vault?"
description: "An open-source, bring-your-own-model alternative to Google NotebookLM that keeps your notebooks in your browser."
---

**better-vault is a free, open-source alternative to Google NotebookLM (now called Gemini Notebook).** You add sources such as PDFs, web pages and notes to a notebook, chat with them and get answers that cite the passages they came from, and turn them into reports, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, slide decks and more. It runs on the AI model you choose: your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google API key, or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio.

Everything you make is stored in your browser. There is no account, no server database and no subscription.

## Who is it for?

- **Students** who want flashcards, quizzes and study guides made from their own course material.
- **Researchers and analysts** who need answers they can check against the source.
- **Teams and self-hosters** who want a NotebookLM-style tool on infrastructure and models they control.
- **Anyone using local models** who wants a private notebook that works with Ollama or LM Studio.

## How is it different from NotebookLM?

NotebookLM runs only on Google's Gemini models, needs a Google account and stores your notebooks on Google's servers. better-vault is MIT-licensed, works with several providers and local models, needs no account and keeps your data on your device. It does not make audio or video overviews. See the [full comparison](/docs/comparison).
