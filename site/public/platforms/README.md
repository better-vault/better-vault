# Platform marks

Logos of the systems the desktop app runs on, shown on the download page.
Downloaded from [theSVG](https://thesvg.org) (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025
thesvg.org). Each mark is a trademark of its owner and is used only to say
that the app runs on that system; it implies no endorsement.

`apple-light.svg` and `apple-dark.svg` are the same mark with its fill set to
black and white, for the light and dark themes. `windows.svg` and `linux.svg`
are the full-colour marks and work on both.
