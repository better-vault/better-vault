A **Report** is a written document made from your selected sources. It reads like a document, with a table of contents, and its claims cite your sources.

## Options

- **Format:**
  - **Briefing doc**: an overview of your sources with key insights and quotes.
  - **Study guide**: a short-answer quiz, essay questions and a glossary.
  - **Blog post**: the most insightful takeaways in a readable article.
  - **Create your own**: describe the structure, style and tone you want.
  - Suggested formats, written for your sources by the model.
- **Length:** short, standard or long.
- **Instructions:** anything extra the model should follow (up to 4,000 characters).

## Download

Word (`.docx`, with citations as footnotes) or Markdown (`.md`).
