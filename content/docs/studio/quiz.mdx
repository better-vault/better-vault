title: "Quiz"
description: "Generate a multiple-choice quiz from your sources, with hints, explanations and a score."
---

A **Quiz** is a set of multiple-choice questions about your sources.

## Options

- **Number of questions:** fewer (5), standard (10) or more (20).
- **Difficulty:** easy, medium or hard.

## Taking the quiz

Each question has a hint you can reveal, and after you answer, an explanation of the right answer. Your score is shown at the end.

## Download

Word (questions, then an answer key on its own page) or Markdown.
