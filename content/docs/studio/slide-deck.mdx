A **Slide deck** is a presentation made from your sources.

## Options

- **Format:**
  - **Detailed deck**: full sentences and the key facts, to read without a speaker.
  - **Presenter slides**: short points on the slides, and what to say in the speaker notes.
- **Length:** short or default.

Slides use layouts such as title, section, bullets, two columns, comparison, stats, quote, timeline and closing.

## Presenting

Present the deck full screen and move between slides with the keyboard.

## Download

PowerPoint (`.pptx`, with speaker notes) or PDF (through your browser's print dialog).
