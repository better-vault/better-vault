---
title: "Whiteboard"
description: "A hand-drawn style board for your own diagrams, or to draw over a mind map, Anatomy figure or infographic."
---

The **Whiteboard** is a blank board with shapes, arrows, a pen, text and pictures, drawn in a hand-drawn style. It needs no model and no sources, and opens at once from **Create**.

- The board fills the Studio tab. Its edits are saved as you go.
- **Annotate** opens a mind map, Anatomy figure or infographic on a new board so you can draw over it.
- Colours follow light and dark mode.
- Open **Keyboard shortcuts** on the board to see them all (⌘ is Ctrl on Windows and Linux).

## Download

PNG, SVG, or the board file (`.json`).
